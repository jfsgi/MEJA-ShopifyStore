Made-to-order policy & FAQ

Everything we make is built by hand for your exact space. Here’s how that works — and what it means for orders.

Made to order — no returns

Because every drawer box, door, unit, and shelf is custom-built to your specifications, all sales are final — we don’t accept returns or exchanges. Before anything is built, you approve a photoreal 4K render of your exact piece, so there are no surprises.

🛈 You approve it before we build it. Your configured 4K render and build plan are attached to every order — what you approve is what we make.

Defects & remakes

We stand behind our craftsmanship. If a piece arrives damaged or not as your approved 4K render, we’ll repair, rework, or remake it at no cost — just contact us within 14 days of delivery with photos.

Frequently asked

How do I measure?

For drawer boxes, measure your cabinet opening width, depth, and height; we account for slide clearances. Our measuring guide walks through each product, and you can preview the fit in 4K before ordering.

How long does it take?

Each piece is built to order and typically ships in 1–3 weeks. Larger drawer units or custom Atelier3D pieces may take longer — your estimated ship window shows at checkout.

What woods & finishes can I choose?

Solid hardwoods — Walnut, White Oak, Cherry, Maple, and Ash — with Matte, Satin, or Hand-oiled finishes. Drawer boxes can be dovetail, half-blind, or box-joint; doors come in slab, shaker, or raised-panel.

How does shipping work?

We ship throughout the continental US, carefully packed. Tracking is emailed when your order leaves the shop; rates are calculated at checkout.

Can I change my order after buying?

Because we start building quickly, contact us as soon as possible. If production hasn’t started we’ll do our best to adjust; once a piece is in the shop it can’t be changed.
